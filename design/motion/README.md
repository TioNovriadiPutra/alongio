# Alongio motion pilots

This index records the Phase 11A prototypes and their evidence. For the current Tiny Planner review, see the [Phase 11B.1R expressive motion prototype](tiny-planner-11b1r.md). The [Phase 11B.1 audit](tiny-planner-11b1.md) records the previous pass. For motion design and handoff, use the [Soft Playful Motion architecture](../MOTION.md) and [animation asset policy](../animations/README.md).

Open **01 — Alongio Motion System** in the [editable FigCraft master](../Alongio.figcraft). Yellow **START** labels mark interactive entries. Supporting states, overlays, and storyboards sit separately. In the presenter, choose a flow beginning with **11A.2**.

| Pilot | Start in the presenter | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | **11A.2 A · Button** | Hold Continue and release inside it. Choose Try again to repeat. |
| B · Date | **11A.2 B · Date · 390 × 844** | Open Date, then Close. Reopen and choose Use November 19. |
| B · Time | **11A.2 B · Time · 390 × 844** | Open Time, then Close. Reopen and choose Use 7:00 PM. |
| C | **11A.2 C · Task completion** | Scroll to Dinner together if needed, check it, then choose Undo. |
| D | **11A.2 D · Couple celebration** | Tap Open Us to play. After the settle, tap the Back arrow to replay or Open Us to continue. |
| E | **11A.2 E · Home to Planner** | Tap the yellow Tiny Planner tile. Select Home from the Planner dock to return. |

Date and Time each have separate 320 × 568 and 440 × 956 flows on the testing board. For reduced-motion playback, choose a flow beginning with **11A.2 Reduced**. The compact and tall reduced picker flows preserve the same sample selections.
