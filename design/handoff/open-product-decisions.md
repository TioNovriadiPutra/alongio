# Alongio open product decisions

The [Phase 9 readiness assessment](implementation-readiness.md#open-product-decisions) classifies these decisions by implementation impact and adds three continuity decisions. Its MVP behaviors are proposals for owner approval.

These are recommendations for owner review. They describe product behavior to confirm before the related prototype paths or application behavior are defined. The [user-flow audit](user-flow-audit.md) explains which journeys each decision affects.

| ID | Priority | Decision | Recommendation and rationale | Affected screens | Implementation implications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PD-01 | HIGH | Can users change their account email? | Keep the verified email visible and immutable for MVP 1 unless recovery and verification are agreed. If editing is included, keep the current sign-in address active until the new one is verified, and give users a clear recovery path if verification fails. This avoids locking people out halfway through the change. | [Account FC-30811](screen-inventory.md#fc-30811), [Email verification FC-892](screen-inventory.md#fc-892), [Verified email FC-1360](screen-inventory.md#fc-1360), [Login FC-808](screen-inventory.md#fc-808), [Password reset FC-997](screen-inventory.md#fc-997) | If included, specify re-authentication, pending address display, verification expiry, cancel/retry, duplicate address handling and session continuity. |
| PD-02 | BLOCKER | What is Personal Mode when a user has a partner? | Treat the chosen view, connection status and access to specific content as separate decisions. Recommend showing private-only content in the Personal view, retaining the partner connection, and requiring an explicit switch to see shared content. Approve a mode-appropriate signed-in Home outcome for new and returning users. This preserves the distinction already shown between switching modes and disconnecting. | [Mode choices FC-1072](screen-inventory.md#fc-1072) and [FC-1410](screen-inventory.md#fc-1410), [Home FC-2](screen-inventory.md#fc-2), [Personal Planner FC-5710](screen-inventory.md#fc-5710), [Personal Me with partner FC-30654](screen-inventory.md#fc-30654), [Mode settings FC-30755](screen-inventory.md#fc-30755), [Connected Us FC-24250](screen-inventory.md#fc-24250) | Define defaults after signup/login, persisted mode, Home and tab destinations by mode, what Us shows in Personal view, and whether an authorized shared deep link prompts a mode change or offers a safe return. Never infer content permission from the selected tab or mode. |
| PD-03 | HIGH | What happens to shared content after partner disconnection? | Recommend removing former-partner access to previously shared content at disconnection and retaining existing private content for its owner. Decide an explicit ownership and archive rule for jointly created plans, routines, achievement history and meals before choosing copy about retention. This protects private memories without promising loss or access that the product has not decided. | [Manage connection FC-24859](screen-inventory.md#fc-24859), [Disconnect FC-24911](screen-inventory.md#fc-24911), [Disconnected FC-24984](screen-inventory.md#fc-24984), [Partner memory FC-12438](screen-inventory.md#fc-12438), [Shared meals FC-16824](screen-inventory.md#fc-16824), [Together Growth FC-5290](screen-inventory.md#fc-5290), [Unshared update FC-33109](screen-inventory.md#fc-33109) | Define revocation timing, offline/cache handling, historical data ownership, notification sanitization, and whether reconnecting restores any sharing. Clarify what stays visible to each person before confirmation. |
| PD-04 | HIGH | Account deletion, shared data and retention | Keep the final deletion action disabled in the approved prototype until the owner confirms eligibility, timing and shared-data treatment. Recommend recent re-authentication and a direct consequences summary before the final confirmation. Decide separately how authored entries, shared artifacts, partner access and notifications are handled. | [Account FC-30811](screen-inventory.md#fc-30811), [Delete account FC-31165](screen-inventory.md#fc-31165), [Disconnect FC-24911](screen-inventory.md#fc-24911), [Inaccessible update FC-33075](screen-inventory.md#fc-33075) | Specify what is deleted, anonymized or retained by data type, who can still see it, whether recovery is offered, and what the other partner sees. Define completion, failure, sign-out and any required review of deletion messaging. |
| PD-05 | MEDIUM | Does opening a notification mark it read? | Recommend marking an item read after its authorized destination opens successfully. Keep the explicit single-item and mark-all actions. For unavailable items, preserve unread status until the person dismisses or explicitly marks the update read. This separates an attempted open from a successful one. | [Inbox FC-31896](screen-inventory.md#fc-31896), [Single-item actions FC-32229](screen-inventory.md#fc-32229), [One marked read FC-32340](screen-inventory.md#fc-32340), [All read FC-32433](screen-inventory.md#fc-32433), [Deleted fallback FC-33041](screen-inventory.md#fc-33041) | Define per-user read state, cross-device behavior, retries and pagination, plus how counters behave after manual actions and after returning from a destination. |
| PD-06 | MEDIUM | How long are updates retained? | Use a bounded retention window for routine inbox events, disclosed in settings if it affects user expectations. Keep event cards distinct from source records and sanitize previews promptly when access changes. Decide the duration and whether security notices have a separate policy based on volume and support needs. | [Inbox FC-31896](screen-inventory.md#fc-31896), [History FC-32088](screen-inventory.md#fc-32088), [Growth history FC-32159](screen-inventory.md#fc-32159), [Unavailable examples FC-33143](screen-inventory.md#fc-33143) | Define expiry rules, grouping and pagination, stale or missing records, account-notice treatment, and retention of the unread count. |
| PD-07 | MEDIUM | What are the default notification preferences? | Separate in-app updates from device push permission. Recommend enabling reminders only when the user sets or opts into them and asking before enabling optional shared and achievement push categories. Keep account-security communication separate from routine preference controls. Clearly explain when device permission is off. | [Preferences FC-31270](screen-inventory.md#fc-31270), [Profile permission FC-31408](screen-inventory.md#fc-31408), [Device off FC-31492](screen-inventory.md#fc-31492), [Little Updates permission FC-32859](screen-inventory.md#fc-32859), [Permission denied FC-32970](screen-inventory.md#fc-32970) | Confirm each category’s default and delivery channel, per-user ownership, settings persistence, duplicate prompt handling, and what changes when shared access is lost. |
| PD-08 | HIGH | What access and return behavior should deep links have? | Recommend checking session and item permissions before displaying a destination, then restoring the originating route or offering a safe Home/inbox fallback. Preserve explicit Diary sharing and use generic notification copy for shared entries. This keeps protected content out of previews and preserves orientation when reopening the app. | [Login FC-808](screen-inventory.md#fc-808), [Task detail FC-2122](screen-inventory.md#fc-2122), [Diary visibility FC-12127](screen-inventory.md#fc-12127), [Inbox FC-31896](screen-inventory.md#fc-31896), [Deleted FC-33041](screen-inventory.md#fc-33041), [Inaccessible FC-33075](screen-inventory.md#fc-33075), [Unshared FC-33109](screen-inventory.md#fc-33109) | Define signed-out, wrong-account, deleted, inaccessible and expired outcomes. Specify what caller and safe target state are restored after authorization. |
| PD-09 | MEDIUM | Can invitees decline a partner invitation explicitly? | Keep sender cancellation, expired codes and invalid codes as distinct outcomes. Decide whether explicit recipient decline belongs in MVP 1. If it does, use neutral sender feedback and preserve Personal Mode for both people. The current approved flow shows pending, successful connection, cancellation, a bad code and expiry. | [Pending FC-24576](screen-inventory.md#fc-24576), [Confirm partner FC-24752](screen-inventory.md#fc-24752), [Invalid code FC-24688](screen-inventory.md#fc-24688), [Expired FC-24725](screen-inventory.md#fc-24725), [Canceled FC-25136](screen-inventory.md#fc-25136) | Define who can act on an invite, what each actor sees, whether codes can be reused, and how to recover from a canceled or expired invitation. |

**Recommended review order.** Resolve PD-02 and PD-08 before wiring the root navigation. Resolve PD-03 and PD-04 before authoring final disconnect and deletion consequences. Resolve PD-05, PD-06 and PD-07 together so the inbox, read state and device choices give users consistent feedback.
