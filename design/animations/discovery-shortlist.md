## Licensing and verification

- **LSL** means the listing identifies the asset as free under the Lottie Simple License, FL 9.13.21. The license permits commercial use and redistribution in an app, including derivatives, provided the asset stays under the same terms and its distribution contains those terms. Creator credit is optional and encouraged. Preserve the license terms with the asset and avoid imposing additional restrictions on it. [Lottie Simple License](https://lottiefiles.com/page/license) · [LottieFiles licensing guidance](https://help.lottiefiles.com/animation-licensing-basics-)
- **MIT** means the source permits commercial use and redistribution under MIT. Retain the applicable copyright and permission notice with distributed copies. A separate in-app author credit is unnecessary under the stated terms. [useAnimations library and license](https://github.com/useAnimations/react-useanimations)
- All rows have a publicly listed free download route and a named standard Lottie JSON option. Numbers are listing-reported standard JSON sizes, excluding any dependencies. Download entitlement for the owner’s workspace and actual transfer are **NOT VERIFIED**. Use the authorized download route and respect its current limits. [LottieFiles Starter plan download limits](https://help.lottiefiles.com/hc/en-us/articles/16240626895385-File-Upload-and-Download-Limits-on-Free-Workspace)
- **Canvas aspect ratio and native React Native compatibility are NOT VERIFIED for every row.** The listings do not establish actual composition dimensions. Confirm iOS and Android rendering after approval. Transparency is also **NOT VERIFIED for every row**, including where a preview would appear on white. File size is **NOT VERIFIED** where the publisher does not state it.
