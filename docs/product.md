# Alongio — Product Overview

## Product Vision

Make everyday life more organized, meaningful, and enjoyable, both individually and together.

## Product Category

Personal and couple lifestyle management mobile application.

## Target Audience

- Gen Z
- Millennials
- Gen Alpha, where age-appropriate

## User Modes

**Personal Mode**

A private space for managing personal activities, responsibilities, and daily life.

**Couple Mode**

A shared space for planning and managing life together.

## User Experience

Alongio should make everyday responsibilities feel lighter and more enjoyable.

The experience should be friendly, playful, and emotionally engaging without overwhelming users with information.

## Potential Feature Domains

- Personal and shared finances
- Tasks and household responsibilities
- Reminders and planning
- Kitchen and meal planning
- Shared moments and memories

These domains represent potential product directions, not finalized MVP requirements.

## Current Objective

Establish Alongio's visual identity, design system, and master home screen before defining and designing the complete MVP.

No application implementation is permitted during this phase.
